A chapel turned home

The studio occupies a former chapel on an estate known for its architecture. The ribbed vaults and bare stone have been kept as they were, and the thick walls keep it cool all summer.

The estate is quiet and wooded, yet shops and restaurants are two minutes away. Arrive when you like and come and go as you please.

A day at La Chapelle

  1. 8 am

    Coffee beneath the vaults

    Morning light comes in through the tall window. Coffee machine, tea and kettle are ready.

  2. 10 am

    The Canal du Midi by bike

    The canal runs 500 metres from the estate. Its towpath leads to Bram or towards Carcassonne; bike hire nearby.

  3. 12 pm

    Market and lunch

    Shops, a butcher-deli and restaurants two minutes away. On Thursdays, an open-air market until noon.

  4. 3 pm

    Lake Bram

    Five minutes away, a lake with a water-sports centre for the afternoon.

  5. 7 pm

    The Cité of Carcassonne

    A 15-minute drive to watch the ramparts light up at dusk.

  6. 11 pm

    Back to the cool

    The thick walls keep the chapel cool, even in high summer.
