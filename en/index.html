Welcome

A chapel turned home

La Chapelle is a 50 m² studio set in a former chapel, in a wooded estate known for its architecture. The ribbed vaults and bare stone have been kept as they were.

The thick walls keep it cool all summer. The estate is quiet, yet shops and restaurants are two minutes away.

FAQ

Good to know before you book

How many guests can stay at La Chapelle?

The studio sleeps up to 4: a 160 cm double bed and a sofa bed for two.

How far is the Cité of Carcassonne?

The Cité of Carcassonne is about 15 minutes by car. The Canal du Midi is 500 metres from the estate, Castelnaudary 20 minutes.

Is there parking?

Yes, free private parking inside the estate.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Self check-in from 5 pm with a key box, check-out by 10 am.

What is the minimum stay?

The minimum stay is 2 nights.

Is linen provided?

Yes: bed linen, towels and tea towels are provided, along with coffee and tea.

Can we use the estate's pool?

No, the estate is shared and the pools are not open to guests.

How do I book direct?

Pick your dates in the calendar and send your request: Conciergerie Lauragaise confirms availability and price and sends you the rental agreement. Payment by bank transfer.
